\# Financial Sensitivity Analysis and Interpretation



\## 1. Purpose



This document summarizes the financial sensitivity analysis of the Armenia M1/M2 directed-network EV fast-charging case study. It interprets the 432 financial sensitivity combinations and 108 break-even demand-realization cases.



The analysis is a preliminary screening exercise, not a bankable financial feasibility study.



\## 2. Reference Assumptions



The reference comparison uses:



\- Demand realization: 100%

\- Fixed O\&M: 4% of CAPEX per year

\- Project life: 15 years

\- Discount rate: 10%



The financial sensitivity analysis varies:



\- Demand realization: 25%, 50%, 75%, and 100%

\- Fixed O\&M: 2%, 4%, and 6% of CAPEX per year

\- Project life: 10, 15, and 20 years

\- Discount rate: 8%, 10%, and 12%



This produces 108 combinations per demand scenario and 432 combinations across all four scenarios.



\## 3. NPV Sensitivity at Reference Financial Assumptions



| Scenario | 25% demand | 50% demand | 75% demand | 100% demand |

|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|

| Low | -1,663,721 | -1,113,620 | -563,519 | -13,418 |

| Base | -772,631 | 2,411,812 | 5,596,255 | 8,780,698 |

| High | 1,866,988 | 15,200,621 | 28,534,254 | 41,867,888 |

| Armstat | -635,157 | 3,088,988 | 6,813,134 | 10,537,279 |



All NPV values are in USD.



At the reference assumptions, the Low scenario remains slightly negative even at 100% demand realization. The Base and Armstat scenarios become positive at 50% demand realization. The High scenario remains positive at 25% demand realization.



\## 4. Positive-NPV Sensitivity Combinations



| Scenario | Positive-NPV cases | Total cases | Share |

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Low | 12 | 108 | 11.11% |

| Base | 84 | 108 | 77.78% |

| High | 102 | 108 | 94.44% |

| Armstat | 85 | 108 | 78.70% |



These shares describe the tested parameter combinations only. They are not statistical probabilities of project success.



The High scenario has the largest number of positive-NPV combinations within the tested parameter grid. This finding should be interpreted together with the high-demand assumptions underlying that scenario.



\## 5. Break-Even Demand Realization



At the reference assumptions of 4% fixed O\&M, 15 years of project life, and a 10% discount rate, the demand realization required for NPV to reach zero is:



| Scenario | Break-even demand realization |

|---|---:|

| Low | 100.61% |

| Base | 31.07% |

| High | 21.50% |

| Armstat | 29.26% |



The Low scenario requires demand realization above the 100% screening demand, indicating that it does not reach financial break-even under the reference assumptions.



The Base, High, and Armstat scenarios have lower break-even demand thresholds within the tested model.



\## 6. Interpretation



The sensitivity results indicate that financial outcomes are strongly dependent on realized charging demand.



The Low scenario is financially fragile under the reference assumptions. The Base and Armstat scenarios show positive NPV at 50% demand realization. The High scenario remains positive at the lowest tested demand realization of 25%.



A higher number of positive-NPV combinations does not prove that a scenario is more likely to occur. Scenario demand assumptions must be considered separately from the financial calculation.



\## 7. Methodological Limitation



The demand-realization sensitivity changes the energy contribution while retaining the selected station layout and CAPEX proxy for each demand scenario. The MILP layout is not re-optimized for each lower demand-realization factor.



Therefore, the financial sensitivity results evaluate the economics of the fixed scenario layouts under alternative demand realization and financial assumptions. They do not represent an independently optimized infrastructure plan for every demand level.



\## 8. Financial Interpretation Limits



The analysis excludes or simplifies several items, including taxes, financing, working capital, charger replacement, residual values, inflation, demand growth, future exchange-rate changes, and verified site-specific grid connection costs.



Retail and electricity-purchase VAT treatment is not normalized. O\&M percentages are analyst assumptions rather than verified Armenian operating-cost quotations. Capital costs are benchmark-based screening proxies rather than local contractor bids.



The results should therefore be presented as preliminary financial screening indicators, not final project profitability or investment recommendations.



\## 9. Conclusion



Within the tested assumptions, the High scenario demonstrates the greatest financial resilience, while the Low scenario remains marginal or negative under the reference financial assumptions. The Base and Armstat scenarios show positive NPV at 50% demand realization.



These findings support the use of demand and cost sensitivity analysis in the project, while highlighting the need for corridor-wide traffic data, verified local construction and grid costs, site-access checks, and a more complete discounted-cash-flow model before investment-level conclusions can be drawn.
